## 📊 Overview  
This project presents a **comprehensive dashboard** for analyzing brain tumor data.  
It visualizes key insights about patients, tumor characteristics, symptoms, treatments, and demographic trends.  
The dashboard helps identify patterns and correlations between **tumor type, patient demographics, symptoms, family history, and medical treatments**.  

---

## 📈 Key Features  

### 👥 Patient Demographics  
- **Total Patients:** 20,000  
- **Total Male:** 9,984  
- **Total Female:** 10,016  

### 🧩 Filters Available  
- **Tumor Location:** Frontal, Occipital, Parietal, Temporal  
- **Stage:** I, II, III, IV  
- **Symptoms:** Headache, Nausea, Seizures, Vision Issues  

---

## 📉 Dashboard Components  

### 1. **Distribution of Tumor Type**  
Pie chart showing equal distribution:  
- 🟡 **Benign:** 50%  
- 🟠 **Malignant:** 50%  

### 2. **Tumor Type Distribution by Gender**  
Bar chart comparing tumor types across **male and female** patients.  
- Females show a slightly higher count of malignant cases.  

### 3. **MRI Result Distribution by Tumor Type**  
Bar chart representing MRI results categorized as **Positive** or **Negative** for both tumor types.  

### 4. **Surgery Performed vs Tumor Type**  
Compares patients who underwent surgery for **Benign** and **Malignant** tumors.  

### 5. **Age Distribution by Tumor Type**  
Line chart showing how tumor type distribution varies across different age ranges.  

### 6. **Family History Impact on Tumor Type**  
Horizontal bar chart showing the relationship between **family history (Yes/No)** and tumor type incidence.  

### 7. **Radiation Treatment vs Tumor Type**  
Line chart comparing patients who **received or did not receive radiation treatment** by tumor type.  

---

## 🧮 Insights  
- Equal distribution between **benign** and **malignant** tumors.  
- Gender balance is nearly equal, with **slightly more females**.  
- **Surgery and radiation** treatments show distinct trends between tumor types.  
- **Family history** has a stronger correlation with **malignant** cases.
